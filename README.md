## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Automatic Mixing** — Effortlessly create seamless transitions between tracks.
- **CUE Points** — Easily set and recall specific points in your tracks for quick access.
- **Loop Synchronization** — Keep your mixes in perfect time with automatic beat matching.
- **MIDI Mapping** — Control the software using dedicated MIDI hardware for a hands-on experience.
- **Waveform Detection** — Automatically detects the BPM of your tracks for optimal mixing.

### Advanced Features:
- **PerfectSync Technology** — Ensure your mixes are perfectly synchronized for a professional sound.
- **Customizable Skins** — Choose from 6 different skins to personalize your interface.
- **Three Band Equalizer** — Fine-tune your sound with precise control over bass, mid, and treble.
- **Multiple Audio Formats Supported** — Mix MP3, WAV, OGG, and more formats with ease.
